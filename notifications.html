What happened

    Times are when Dashboard Hub noticed a change, in your computer's time zone. It notices changes when it scans your network, so turn on Live update on the Network Map for the most exact times. A device already online when watching began shows "already online". The Uptime view only counts time when Dashboard Hub was really scanning: other time is shown as "not watched", never as offline, and a time given as "sometime between" means Dashboard Hub was closed when it happened. Records are kept for a year and stored only on this computer.
